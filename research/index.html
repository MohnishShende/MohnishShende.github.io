What if the mind we rely on to make decisions is not equally reliable in every state?

The Operating Manual: Meditations on the Mind, Judgment, and Living Well

By Mohnish Shende

A practical, reflective guide to how emotion, stress, mood, exhaustion, uncertainty, and cognitive bias can alter perception and judgment.

Written as a manual to return to when circumstances become difficult, emotions become convincing, or an important decision deserves another examination.

Strong feelings deserve respect, but they do not automatically deserve authority.

  • Separating events from interpretations
  • Stress, attention, and distorted judgment
  • Cognitive bias, fusion, and defense mechanisms
  • Boundaries, trust, conflict, empathy, and manipulation
  • Uncertainty, regret, values, identity, and wisdom
  • Meaning, gratitude, hope, acceptance, and mortality
