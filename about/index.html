About

Engineering practice shaped by explicit evidence.

Mohnish Shende works across cybersecurity and infrastructure engineering, with research as a parallel branch rather than a substitute for technical practice.

Professional focus

The technical anchor is Linux infrastructure, vulnerability assessment, Python security tooling, secure configuration, and clear operational documentation. Public projects include a self-hosted systems handbook, an offline Nmap workflow prototype, and claim-level engineering assurance work.

Evidence-led analysis

The differentiator is a preference for traceable reasoning: separate observations from inference, connect claims to sources, state exclusions, and make uncertainty visible. This is useful in infrastructure assessment, OSINT reporting, assurance, and research alike.

Research in parallel

Research spans forensic psychology, coercive influence, epistemology, and political philosophy. Publication types remain clearly classified, and theoretical work is not presented as empirical validation.

Current boundary

This site is a curated public record. Raw infrastructure audits, live-target reconnaissance, private topology, and rights-restricted full text are intentionally excluded.
