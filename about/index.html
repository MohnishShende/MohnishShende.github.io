About

Different fields. One standard of evidence.

Mohnish Shende is a cybersecurity and infrastructure engineer whose research, investigations, and writing share a disciplined method: distinguish what is observed from what is inferred, connect claims to support, and leave uncertainty visible.

Professional focus

The technical anchor is Linux infrastructure, vulnerability assessment, Python security tooling, secure configuration, and operational documentation. Public projects show systems work, an offline Nmap workflow prototype, and claim-level engineering assurance.

Working method

Across infrastructure assessment, OSINT, assurance, and research, the same discipline applies: preserve provenance, state exclusions, test interpretations against alternatives, and make limitations part of the record.

Parallel branches

Research spans forensic psychology, coercive influence, epistemology, and political philosophy. Investigations and creative writing remain distinct forms, with their own evidence, attribution, and publication boundaries.

Public boundary

This site is a curated public record. Raw infrastructure audits, live-target reconnaissance, private topology, rights-restricted full text, and sensitive source material are intentionally excluded.
