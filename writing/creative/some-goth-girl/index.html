Some Goth Girl

I fell in love with a preacher.

She can’t say my name, but she can pray to him,
for things she can’t ask me for.

I tried to tell her I can’t leave her,
She won’t listen, but she hears everything.

She won’t feel my lips, but she can sin for him,
Graves she can rob, undead she can touch.

My head is full of shit, I can’t hear another sermon,
Of her vile depravity, her crude religion.

I should let her run, or end up in her soup,
Would she preach about my broth?

I lay my head to rest, and try my best to sleep,
I want her to be quick, and that is the final mercy I expect.
