The Operating Manual

Meditations on the Mind, Judgment, and Living Well

Mohnish Shende · 10 August 2026 · English · 469 pages

View on Amazon ↗Research context →

What if the mind you rely on to make decisions is not equally reliable in every state?

The book examines how emotion, stress, mood, exhaustion, uncertainty, and cognitive bias can influence perception and judgment. It is written as a manual to return to when circumstances become difficult, emotions become convincing, or an important decision deserves another examination.

Strong feelings deserve respect, but they do not automatically deserve authority.

Across six parts

  • Separating events from interpretations.
  • Stress, attention, and distorted judgment.
  • Cognitive distortions, confirmation bias, fusion, and defense mechanisms.
  • Boundaries, trust, conflict, empathy, and manipulation.
  • Uncertainty, regret, values, habits, motivation, identity, and wisdom.
  • Meaning, gratitude, hope, acceptance, mortality, and living well.
