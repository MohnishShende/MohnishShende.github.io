Work/eci-evm-engineering-assurance

Assurance and evidence

ECI EVM Engineering Assurance

A structured review of public evidence concerning the Election Commission of India EVM ecosystem, organized around traceable claims, exclusions, and validation boundaries.

Substantive v1 repositoryClaim-level technical assessment

View repository ↗

Purpose

Evaluate claims at the level the evidence permits.

This work organizes a large public-document corpus into claims, evidence entries, review packages, and traceability records. Its purpose is assurance-oriented reasoning: identify what a public source supports, what it does not, and which exclusions constrain the conclusion.

Method

Traceability

Source
Public documentation receives an identity and evidentiary role.
Claim
A bounded statement is separated from broader interpretation.
Review
Supporting and limiting material is assessed together.
Exclusion
Firmware, source code, hardware teardown, and live election audit remain outside scope.

Publication state

Repository release, not confirmed SSRN publication.

The public repository is substantive implementation and documentation evidence. It states that an SSRN submission is under editorial review; this portfolio record does not convert that repository claim into a confirmed posting or publication.

Public boundary

What this page does not claim

The repository reports SSRN editorial review; publication or posting has not been independently confirmed. Firmware, source, teardown, and live election audit are excluded.
